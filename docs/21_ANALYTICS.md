## Critical metrics

### Retention
D1, D7, D30

### Economy
- Coins per session;
- Coins/sec progression;
- clicks/session;
- Pack openings;
- Pack affordability;
- Essence generated/spent;
- duplicate ratio;
- offline claims.

### Collection
- unique cards;
- Set completion;
- cards/session;
- discovery rate;
- time to first Rare;
- time to first Epic;
- time to first Set completion.

### Idle balance
- percentage of income from clicking;
- percentage from automation;
- percentage from Sets;
- offline income share.

The intended long-term trend is that clicking becomes a smaller percentage of income while collection/idle systems become stronger.

## Product questions

- Does the player understand how to earn their next Pack?
- Are duplicates frustrating?
- Are Sets motivating?
- Is passive income exciting?
- Is the clicker useful without becoming tedious?
- Are players exploring both Games and Anime?
