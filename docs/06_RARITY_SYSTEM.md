## Principle

Rarity is a CARDVERSE property.

External ratings are inputs, not final rarity.

## Candidate score

Inputs may include:

- popularity;
- user rating;
- critical reception;
- historical/legacy value;
- age;
- uniqueness;
- franchise importance;
- editorial score.

## Example weighting — Games

Popularity 30%
Rating 20%
Critical reception 15%
Legacy 15%
Age 10%
Uniqueness 10%

## Example weighting — Anime

Popularity 25%
Rating 25%
Legacy 15%
Franchise importance 15%
Longevity 10%
Uniqueness 10%

## Candidate thresholds

0–24 Common
25–44 Uncommon
45–64 Rare
65–79 Epic
80–92 Legendary
93–97 Mythic
98–100 Secret

These are starting values, not final balance.

## Editorial overrides

Use when source metrics fail to represent cultural importance.

Store:

- rarity_override;
- reason;
- reviewer;
- timestamp.

## Pack probabilities

Example standard distribution:

Common 60%
Uncommon 25%
Rare 10%
Epic 4%
Legendary 0.9%
Mythic 0.09%
Secret 0.01%

The actual configuration must be simulated before release.

## First-pack exception

The first Discovery Pack may use a special onboarding configuration, including a guaranteed Epic.

Do not let this contaminate the standard Pack economy.
