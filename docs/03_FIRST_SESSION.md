# 03 — First Session / Onboarding

This document defines exactly what happens when a completely new player enters CARDVERSE.

## Goal

The player must understand the core fantasy and open their first cards within approximately one minute.

## Step 1 — Welcome

Screen:

**WELCOME TO CARDVERSE**

"Games. Anime. Infinite things to collect."

Button:

**START COLLECTION**

No account is required.

## Step 2 — Tiny introduction

Show several cards for 3–5 seconds.

Explain only:

- Cards represent real games/anime and related entities.
- Cards have different rarities.
- Sets can be completed.
- Completed Sets eventually generate passive Coins.

Do not dump all systems on the player.

## Step 3 — Free Discovery Pack

Immediately grant:

**BEGINNER DISCOVERY PACK**

No work is required.

Button:

**OPEN PACK**

## Step 4 — First Pack

The first Pack is scripted enough to create a strong first impression.

Recommended:

- 5 cards.
- At least 2–3 new cards.
- At least one Rare.
- One guaranteed Epic.
- No Mythic/Secret guarantee.

The exact distribution must be configurable.

## Step 5 — Collection reveal

After opening:

"You discovered X new cards."

Button:

**VIEW COLLECTION**

Show the first set and missing slots.

The player should immediately see:

- owned cards;
- missing cards;
- rarity;
- set progress.

## Step 6 — Introduce the second Pack

After the collection screen, present:

**YOUR NEXT PACK**

Give the player a second Pack or a direct Pack Ticket so they can open it without a long grind.

Purpose: teach the player that packs are repeatable and collection is the goal.

## Step 7 — Introduce the Clicker

After the second opening:

"You've seen the collection. Now build the machine that funds it."

Show:

- Coins;
- Coins per click;
- Coins per second;
- Pack cost.

Example:

**Discovery Pack — 100 Coins**

The player starts with enough starter Coins to make the first clicker interaction meaningful.

## Step 8 — First earning task

Quest:

**Earn 100 Coins**

Reward:

- access to the next Pack, or
- enough Coins to buy it.

This prevents the player from encountering the problem:

"I need a Pack to complete a quest, but I have no Pack."

The economic engine must always bootstrap itself.

## Step 9 — First duplicate

The second/third Pack should have a reasonable chance of producing a duplicate.

Explain:

**Duplicate → Essence**

Do not punish the player with a complicated conversion screen.

## Step 10 — First upgrade

Allow one cheap upgrade.

Example:

Duplicate + Essence → Card Level 2.

Explain visually that upgraded cards become more valuable/prestigious.

## Step 11 — Introduce Sets

Show:

**SET: GAMING ORIGINS**

Example:

23 / 50 cards.

Explain:

"Complete Sets to unlock passive income."

Do not require a complete Set during onboarding.

## Step 12 — First idle income

Give the player a tiny starter Set or scripted milestone that unlocks a low passive income source.

Example:

**Starter Collection Bonus: +1 Coin/sec**

Then explain:

"Your collection now works while you're away."

## Step 13 — End onboarding

Message:

**NOW YOU'RE A COLLECTOR.**

The player receives:

- daily quests;
- collection goals;
- Game/Anime choice;
- first level progression.

From this point the game stops being scripted.

## Recommended first 15 minutes

00:00 Start
00:30 Introduction
01:00 First free Pack
02:00 Collection
03:00 Second Pack
05:00 Clicker
06:00 Earn Coins
07:00 Third Pack
08:00 Duplicate → Essence
09:00 Upgrade
10:00 Sets
11:00 First passive income
12:00 Player Level
13:00 Daily quests
14:00 Game/Anime collection choice
15:00 Free exploration

## Critical onboarding rule

Never require a player to perform a task whose prerequisite resource they do not possess.

The first economic loop must be self-starting.
