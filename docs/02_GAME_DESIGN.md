# 02 — Game Design

## Core loop

**EARN → OPEN → COLLECT → COMPLETE → PRODUCE → EARN**

Detailed:

1. Click or wait for idle income.
2. Receive Coins.
3. Spend Coins on a Pack or earn a Pack Ticket.
4. Open the Pack.
5. Receive Cards.
6. Add new Cards to the Collection.
7. Convert duplicates into Essence.
8. Upgrade selected Cards.
9. Complete Sets.
10. Completed Sets generate passive Coins.
11. Passive Coins fund more Packs.
12. New Sets and Events create new collection goals.

## Secondary loop

Discover content → inspect related entities → complete themed sets → achievements → rewards → stronger collection economy.

## Player motivation

### Collector
"Complete every card in a set."

### Rare hunter
"Find Legendary/Mythic/Secret cards."

### Explorer
"Discover games and anime I don't know."

### Idle player
"Build a collection that earns Coins automatically."

### Completionist
"Master sets and collect variants."

### Social player
"Showcase my collection and compare milestones."

## No-PvP rule

Cards are never balanced for combat.

Stats communicate identity and rarity and may support quests, achievements, collection challenges or future non-combat systems.

## Economy should support collection

The player should not spend most of the session clicking.

Clicking is strongest at the beginning, then automation and completed sets gradually dominate.
