# 01 — Project Overview

## Vision

CARDVERSE is a collectible idle game where a player's collection is also their economic engine.

The player discovers games and anime, obtains cards, completes sets, upgrades cards, and turns completed collections into passive income.

## Design pillars

1. Collection
2. Discovery
3. Idle progression
4. Meaningful scarcity
5. Completion
6. Fair free progression
7. Long-term content
8. Multi-universe architecture

## Product fantasy

The desired feeling is:

> "I am building an enormous personal archive of games and anime, and the more complete it becomes, the more powerful my collection economy gets."

## What the game is not

- Not PvP.
- Not a traditional card battler.
- Not a gambling product.
- Not an NFT project.
- Not primarily an anime/game tracker.
- Not dependent on third-party APIs at runtime.
